# IIST Open Source Society

The IIST Open Source Society (IIST-OSS) is a student community at the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology (IIST), Thiruvananthapuram, dedicated to open-source software development, collaboration, and learning.

## Mission

To foster a culture of open collaboration at IIST by building and maintaining open-source projects, supporting students in making their first contributions, and connecting the campus with the wider open-source ecosystem.

## Activities

- **Projects:** Student-led open-source software, hosted and maintained in this organization.
- **Events:** Talks, workshops, and contribution drives open to the IIST community.
- **Mentorship:** Guidance for students new to version control, code review, and open-source practices.
- **Project proposals:** A structured process for students to propose new projects and seek support.
